# CSS reflection
## Why we did it in this way?
We designed our CSS in order to be visually interesting but also easy to do, for that reason in the header we only added a paw icon and the "Raise a Pncho" name to it, on the body we first putted a grid for the 3 options of "Adopt a Pancho", "Take care of your Pancho", or "Are you a teacher? Enter de dog park" and its respective image on a *Grid* cause it is easy for manipulating the elements horizontally and vertically, reason why we decided to include it here cause we neede to be careful with the margin and the introductory next on top of these options. At the part of read more we used flex box to help us center the option in a much easier way. We used the sticky position for the users to continue seeing our header to return to the main page any time they want, if we would choosed the fixed position, then either the user scrolls or changes the frame where the header is, he is going to loose the sight of the header and, for consequent, of the homepage.

Talking about the rest of decision, in this CSS we wanted to show a child friendly design, thought as a eye catching web for them to get engaged to the autonomous study. One of the outcomes of this thought is the main character in our web: Pancho the sausage dog; who is designed as a cute pet with minimalist design. The 2 Panchos are to aid the users understand to what part that section is going to bring them to, the dog park image helps uderstand the teacher that there is where he is goign to see all of his students dogs.

We selected the pastel analogous color palette, that consists of pastel blue, pastel green, and pastel turqoise as an attempt to be soft and cool for the sight. This will be useful for the users to not get tired that easy of looking at our page due to the cool colours, it also is pretty comfortable for young teenagers. This will guarantee for the students to dont have the bad first impression of another boring study website, but an entretaining one.

We leaved the divisions as it is shown because we wanted make it easy and intuitive to acces to the other parts of the raise a Pancho website, having all the 4 parts agrouped help to dont make the people get dispersed and loose the interest on the website; and having it as a eye catching way make it a lot easier. As it is already known, all our website is themed on dogs, so the icon, the images representing the other parts of the website, and the decoration in general (looking like a dog park) was applied in that way to make it clear our website is for young students that only want to have fun while studying due that dogs are something very heart-touching for people of that age. For the teachers we didn´t make it that childish, but very eye-catching nevertheless.
